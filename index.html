CONTROLS

Move

Left thumb anywhere on the left side. Push to the edge to sprint, or hold SPRINT.

Pass

Tap = low pass to the player you aim at. Flick up = high pass.

Through

Tap = ball into space. Flick up = lobbed over the defence.

Shoot

Hold for power and release. While holding, swipe chip · low driven (aim with the joystick) · power shot · fake shot.

Skill

Tap step-over · La Croqueta · rainbow flick · drag-back.

Defend

SWITCH player · TACKLE · SLIDE tackle · hold SPRINT to press the ball carrier (he closes him down, then contains) · hold 2ND PRESS to send a teammate too.

Set pieces

Free kick in range: aim with the stick, hold SHOOT and release to curl it over the wall (more power = harder to dip), or PASS it short. Penalty: aim at a corner, hold & release SHOOT. Mistimed and from-behind tackles give fouls and cards; a second yellow is a red. Offside is on (switch it off in Settings); pass assist looks for an onside team-mate.

One-touch

Press PASS or SHOOT while a pass is coming to you to play it first time.

Keyboard

WASD · Space/U pass · K shoot (O chip · N low · M power · B fake) · L/Y through · Shift sprint · E skill · P pause.
